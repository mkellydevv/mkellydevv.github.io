About me

Software engineer with experience building distributed systems and cloud infrastructure at Google Cloud, where I worked on large-scale on-boarding, provisioning, compliance, and reliability initiatives.

Since leaving Google, I've been building software products independently, taking projects from product strategy and architecture through development, testing, and deployment while managing the technical side of an early-stage business.

I enjoy working across the stack, but especially gravitate toward back-end systems, cloud infrastructure, and solving complex engineering problems. I'm particularly interested in building products from the ground up, turning ambiguous ideas into practical systems, and working with small teams where engineers can have meaningful ownership.

If you would like to know more about me or any of my projects, please feel free to reach out to me through e-mail or on LinkedIn.
